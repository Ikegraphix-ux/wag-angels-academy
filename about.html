About WAG

A school community built around learning.

WAG Angels Academy serves children and families in Kumasi through early years, primary and junior high education.

A message from school leadership

Welcome to WAG Angels Academy.

Our purpose is to provide a supportive place where children can learn, develop confidence and build good relationships. We know each child’s journey is different, and we value working closely with families as they progress through school.

For questions about programmes, campus arrangements or a visit, our team is happy to speak with you.
