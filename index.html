Our Journey

School History (Timeline)

2012
School Founded

WAG Angels Academy opened its doors at Oldsite – Sofoline, Kumasi, with a mission to serve the community through quality basic education.

2015
Expansion of Facilities

New classrooms, a library, and sports facilities were added to accommodate growing enrolment and expanding programmes.

2018
Introduction of JHS

The Junior High School programme launched, completing the full basic school cycle from Pre-School through JHS 3.

2020
Main Campus Launched

A larger Main Campus opened at Newsite – Atwima Takyiman, enabling expansion of BS1–BS9 programmes in a modern facility.

2024
Growing Stronger Together

500+ students across two thriving campuses, with a 98% BECE pass rate and regional quiz championship titles.
