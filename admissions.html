How to enquire

We do not collect applications on this website. Please contact the school to receive current admissions guidance.

  1. 01

    Tell us the class you are considering

    Share your child’s age or current class and ask which campus is appropriate.

  2. 02

    Confirm availability and fees

    Request current information from the school office, including any costs and documents required.

  3. 03

    Arrange a visit or collect the official form

    The school will explain how to meet staff, visit the campus and complete the formal enrolment process.

Speak with the office

Admissions questions are best answered by the school.

Ask about early years, primary or junior high placement, current availability and the right campus for your family.
