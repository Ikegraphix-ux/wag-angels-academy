Before applying

Ask the school for the current requirements.

Requirements can differ by class and may change. Contact the office before preparing documents or making a payment.

Questions to ask

  • Is there space in the class we need?
  • Which campus should we visit?
  • What documents are required?
  • What are the current fees and payment steps?

Admissions process

Four simple steps

  1. 01

    Make an enquiry

    Tell the school your child’s age or current class and the level you are considering.

  2. 02

    Confirm the details

    Ask about space, fees, campus location and the current document list.

  3. 03

    Visit and get the official form

    The office will explain how to meet the team and complete the school’s application.

  4. 04

    Follow the school’s instructions

    Submit the required information and wait for the school to confirm the next step.
