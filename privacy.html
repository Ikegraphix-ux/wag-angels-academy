Privacy notice

Last reviewed: October 2026

Information on this website

This site does not provide an account, application portal or online mailing-list service. Contact and newsletter forms open a draft email in your own email application; this website does not send or store those messages.

If you email or call the school

If you choose to send an email, the information in it is handled by your email provider and the school as part of responding to you. Please do not include sensitive information about a child unless the school has asked you to use an appropriate channel.

Maps and external services

Links to external services such as Google Maps are governed by the privacy practices of those services. This site does not embed a third-party map.

Website hosting

The company hosting this website may process technical information such as server logs. Contact the school if you need information about its current hosting arrangements.

Questions

For privacy questions, contact info@wagangelsacademy.edu.gh or call +233 24 346 2861.
